# Sudoku for Mom

A Sudoku game for one player, made for Mom and played on her phone.

## Language

**Difficulty**:
One of four levels a puzzle belongs to: Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert. It is decided by the hardest Technique needed to solve the puzzle, not by how many clues it has.
_Avoid_: Level, mode

**Technique**:
A named logical deduction a person uses to place a number or rule one out, such as a hidden single or an X-Wing.
_Avoid_: Strategy, rule

**Mistake**:
A number the player entered that does not match the puzzle's one solution. Mistakes show in red right away, unless the player turns that off.
_Avoid_: Error, wrong answer

**Given**:
A number the puzzle starts with. The player cannot change it.
_Avoid_: Clue, starting number

**Candidate**:
A number that could still go in a cell given the numbers already placed in its row, column and box.
_Avoid_: Possibility, option

**Pencil Mark**:
A small note the player writes in a cell to track the Candidates she thinks are possible. It can be wrong or incomplete.
_Avoid_: Note, annotation

**Hint**:
The simplest Technique that makes progress on the current board, shown with the cells involved and an explanation. Applying it places a number or removes Candidates from the Pencil Marks. If the board has a Mistake, the Hint points that out instead.
_Avoid_: Tip, clue
